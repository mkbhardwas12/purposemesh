# Code of conduct

PurposeMesh contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and
productive for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- communicating with empathy, clarity, and technical honesty;
- welcoming different experience levels and viewpoints;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure; and
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and
publishing another person’s confidential information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and project-related community spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and addressed proportionately, including warnings, temporary
restrictions, or permanent removal from project spaces.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
